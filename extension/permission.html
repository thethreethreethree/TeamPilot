Enable image capture

C.A.R.E browser extension

Capturing a conversation saves its text right away. Images shared in the chat are hosted on other sites, so the extension needs your permission to read them.

  • Grants access only so images in a capture can be saved to your workspace.
  • The extension never browses or reads pages on its own — it acts only when you click Capture.
  • You can revoke this any time from the extension's details page in your browser.

After you allow it, close this tab and re-run your capture — images in the thread will now save with it.
