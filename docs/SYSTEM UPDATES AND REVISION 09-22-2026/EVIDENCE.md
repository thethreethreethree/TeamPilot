## A correction to my own reading

Three numbers I stated from the **canvas JPEG** before opening the PDF at full resolution were
wrong, and the PDF is authoritative: buying questions are capped at **+6** (not +8), talking over
the customer at **−6** (not −8), and "not enough questions" triggers below **3** discovery
questions (not 5). The code already had 6, 6 and 3 — it was built from the rubric PDF. The clip
timestamp is **5:12**, not 6:12. [OBSERVED]
