Core Capabilities

Not a tool. An executive system.

ExecOS transforms raw business data into actionable intelligence — so you always know what matters and what to do next.

CEO Command Center

Business health scores, AI executive briefings, daily priorities, and real-time alerts — one command dashboard for decision-makers.

Operations Intelligence

Detect bottlenecks, blocked tasks, and execution failures the moment they happen. AI identifies root causes and recommends action.

Team Intelligence

Workload balance, performance tracking, burnout detection, accountability flags. Know who's overloaded and who's underutilized.

Conversation Intelligence

Paste any meeting transcript or team thread. ExecOS extracts decisions, generates action plans, and assigns tasks automatically.

AI Decision Engine

Describe any situation. Get Safe, Balanced, and Aggressive options with expected outcomes and tradeoffs — powered by Claude.

Decision Memory

Every decision stored, tracked, referenced. The AI surfaces past decisions to flag unexecuted choices and improve over time.
