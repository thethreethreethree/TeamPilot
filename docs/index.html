Core Capabilities

Not a tool. An executive system.

ExecOS transforms raw business data into actionable intelligence — so you always know what matters most and what to do next.

CEO Command Center

Business health scores, AI executive briefings, daily priorities, and real-time alerts — all in one command dashboard built for decision-makers.

Operations Intelligence

Detect bottlenecks, blocked tasks, and execution failures the moment they happen. AI identifies the root cause and recommends immediate action.

Team Intelligence

Workload balance, performance tracking, burnout detection, and accountability flags. Know who's overloaded, who's underutilized, and why.

Conversation Intelligence

Paste any meeting transcript, Slack thread, or email chain. ExecOS extracts decisions, generates action plans, and assigns tasks automatically.

AI Decision Engine

Describe any business situation. Get three structured options — Safe, Balanced, Aggressive — each with expected outcomes and tradeoffs.

Decision Memory

Every decision is stored and tracked. The AI references past decisions to surface patterns, flag unexecuted choices, and improve over time.
