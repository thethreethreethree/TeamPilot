The Browser Extension

Coach every sales conversation — right where it happens.

ELOSTATE Sales Coach is a browser extension that puts an AI sales coach on the conversation you're already looking at. Reading a prospect's reply in Gmail, WhatsApp, LinkedIn or Slack? Click the icon and — without leaving the thread — read the room, grade your draft against the sales playbook, catch up on where the deal stands, or have the next message drafted for you.

◎
What it is
A lightweight browser extension. No new app to learn — it works on the sites your reps already sell in.
↗
What it's for
Turning a real, live conversation into a coached one — in the moment, before the reply is sent.
⛭
How it works
It reads the open thread on the page, sends the text to your ELOSTATE workspace, and shows the coaching in a panel.

How to use it

1
Install it
From your ELOSTATE dashboard → Browser extension, download the package and unzip it. On chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode and click Load unpacked. (A one-click Chrome Web Store install is coming.)
2
Pin it & sign in
Pin the Sales Coach icon to your toolbar. Click it on any page and press Sign in — it connects to your workspace in one click.
3
Open a conversation
Open a real thread — Gmail, WhatsApp Web, LinkedIn, Slack, or any of the 17 supported platforms. On other sites, just highlight the messages.
4
Click the icon → pick a tool
The panel reads the thread and previews what it captured. Choose Read the room, Coach my reply, Catch me up, Draft my reply, or Say it for me — and copy the result straight into your reply.
Coach the deal before you hit send.
Available now for your team — find it under “Browser extension” in the Sales Coach sidebar.
Private by design

The extension reads only the conversation you have open, only when you ask it to. Nothing is captured in the background, and the coaching tools process that text without storing it. Your workspace plan controls access.
