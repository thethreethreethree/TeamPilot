Dissect · worked example

The problem

The customer was billed twice on an auto-renewal and reads it as being overcharged on purpose.

Evidence

Named a specific renewal, not a general complaint“my Pro plan renewed but I was double-charged this month.”

Root cause

Likely a retried payment that captured twice — a systems issue, not a pricing dispute. Diagnose the charge log before offering a refund.

Angles to consider

Confirm both charge IDs · check the renewal retry window · lead with the fix, not an apology loop.

What does the charge log show for this account this cycle?

Dissect separates symptom from root cause, grounded in the actual thread.

Per-skill letter grades — coaching targets, never a leaderboard.

Every conversation becomes coaching material, tailored to the individual. Grades are per-skill and framed as growth areas — there is deliberately no “F”, and cards are never sorted into a ranking. The measure is the person’s own trajectory, not a race against teammates.

Quality is instrumented by consequence — resolution, durability, fewer clarification cycles — not by vanity activity counts.
