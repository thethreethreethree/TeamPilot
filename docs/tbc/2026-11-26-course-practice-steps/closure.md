# CLOSURE - Practice steps in training courses

## What is true now

A course step is a lesson or a practice of one of the company's scenarios. Assigning a course assigns its practice
too, due with the course; adding a practice step later reaches everyone already taking it. The rep sees "Practise it"
on the step, and it counts as done when the practice does (attempted, or passed when the scenario has a pass mark).
The practice screen links back to Training.

Un-named reliance: a rep who already had that scenario keeps their own due date and attempts; the course counts it.
