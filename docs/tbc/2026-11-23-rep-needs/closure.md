# CLOSURE - Rank stage practice by what the rep misses

## What is true now

Managers can tag a practice scenario with the rubric items it trains (Training → "Trains:" → "+ Rubric item"). On a
prospect, the stage's practice is ordered by what that prospect's rep has missed most on their last 10 scored phone
and video calls (a miss counts 1, a partial ½, at least 3 grades), and the first ones say why: "Trains Asks for the
order — missed on 3 of the last 5 scored calls". Untagged scenarios stay listed after them; if the grades can't be
read, the list keeps its order. Migration 0326 is applied.
