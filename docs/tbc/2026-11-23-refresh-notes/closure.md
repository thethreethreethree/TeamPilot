# CLOSURE - Refresh the AI notes after transcript corrections

## What is true now

Once a call's transcript has corrections, the rep or their manager sees "Refresh the AI notes with these corrections"
in the transcript; one click redoes the summary, review and follow-through from the corrected words (after the reply;
reload in a minute). It is refused with no corrections or without the right to edit, logged in the audit log, and
limited to 3 per 10 minutes per person. Nothing re-runs on its own.
