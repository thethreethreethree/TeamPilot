03Framework spine, audit & what’s yours to decide

Built to the spec, governed by the framework — and honest about what isn’t finished.

Framework the build satisfies — as originally reported. Two of these were false when I wrote them.

  • A18 — surfacing rep data to a manager, the label is the defense: Strengths / Growth areas, grades as targets, no F, no leaderboard.
  • A10 — no shadow read: the manager sees the identical numbers the rep sees; same endpoint, same computation.
  • A11 — mirror not verdict: each letter grade travels with its /10; strengths/growth are derived from those counts.
  • §3.5 / §3.6 — no fabricated measure; a thin sample shows “still accumulating”.
  • §3.4 — a failed read shows an error, not zeros (fixed in audit).
  • AMD-006 — built foundation-up: data → APIs → composition → surface; Expert isolation proven by diff.

Honest status

  • Two open not-shippable claims, and one I withdrew. The feature doesn’t deliver (no playback exists) and can’t be measured (it emits no events) — both rest on the framework’s words plus my reading of your spec, and you can overrule either. The third (“surveillance”) was mine and was wrong: guidance already exists elsewhere in the product.
  • BUILT + `npm run check` green — all six gates, exit 0 (typecheck · lint · theme 0 leaks · RLS 0 risks · invariant 0 violations · 857 tests) + build. NOT TESTED live — no route has run against a real DB.
  • Nothing is being deleted. The purge has never run; the “2-day retention” is a read filter. The UI said otherwise until I fixed it — a false privacy assurance.
  • 0187 UNAPPLIED, and possibly moot: if the audio is dropped, it and the purge and Save all go with it.
  • The grade bands are not yours to set — I said they were; that was wrong. They’re §4 instrumentation, answered by data.

Audit result (against the framework, actual files re-read)

  • Fixed — §3.4: manager views rendered fetch failures as empty/wrong content → now an honest error state.
  • Fixed — A19: a stale Standard-Analytics comment → rewritten to the role-conditional behavior.
  • Fixed — the rep couldn’t see the grade you see about them. Found by reading the no-shadow-read clause at its source: it requires the same data at the same level of detail, and the letter + strengths/growth call existed only on your screen. Now on theirs too.
  • Fixed — you were shown the grade without its evidence. The rep’s own card lists the countable behavior; your profile of them listed only the letter. That is backwards — it hands authority a verdict and removes the part you’d actually coach with. The counts now sit under every grade.
  • Fixed — migration-coupling (the Jul-3 outage class, repeated): recordings assumed 0187 was live and would have been hard-down until you applied it. Now degrades to the 2-day window. The degrade-vs-fail-loud rule is pure and unit-pinned — writing those tests corrected it twice (a bare code check would have swallowed a different column going missing; the write path fails with a different code entirely).
  • Your decision ?2 — rep-facing recording-Save UI (spec “or user” + A10). Additive if you want it.
  • Your decision ?3 — unify the two rep views into one profile (my ⑧ recommendation) vs keep them split (closer to your two PDF screenshots). A real refactor either way; your call.
  • Your decision ?7 — decide this one first. Nothing in this product can play a recording. The audio is uploaded and stored, and read by nothing that renders a player. So a manager clicking a recording gets a transcript; Save preserves a file nobody can hear; the 2-day purge deletes one nobody could have heard. The audio is real and piling up regardless — storage and privacy exposure with zero value drawn from it. Either build playback (~60 lines), or stop storing the audio (which deletes 0187, the purge and Save along with it), or ship as-is knowingly.
  • Your decision ?8 — who may un-save? Your PDF names who may save and is silent on this, and I decided it silently: today a rep can un-save what their manager saved, erasing who saved it, and the purge then deletes it. Harmless while nothing can play the audio — destructive the moment ?7 builds playback. If you build playback, rule on this first.
  • Your decision ?5 — I recommend letting the COUNT be the label. Not the letter, and not the letter paired with a word (my earlier answer). Your own C.A.R.E audit diagnosed this a month ago: a 3-tier word ladder “invites the leader to rank agents — which IS comparison”, and it prescribed labels “descriptive of the reply shape, not of agent worth”. A 9-tier letter ladder is that amplified, and “D” carries zero information about what happened. The label it asks for is already in the build — the count I added under each grade: “Closing — asked for the close in 2 of the last 9 calls”. The letter adds nothing the count doesn’t, and adds the one thing your framework rejects. (That audit was written by a previous agent session, not by you — so it is corroboration, not you contradicting yourself. I originally told you it was “your own audit”, which overstated my case in my favour.)
