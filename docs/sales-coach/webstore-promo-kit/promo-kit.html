5 · Assembly checklist

  • Generate the 6 clips (one per scene) with the matching storyboard frame attached as a reference.
  • Stitch in order 1→6 to hit ~30s; trim each to keep the pace tight (the reply scene can run a touch longer).
  • One music bed across all six; ride the VO under it. Keep captions on (accessibility + muted autoplay).
  • Export 1920×1080 MP4, upload to YouTube, paste the URL into the store's "Global promo video" field.
  • Store images: Screenshots 1–5 at 1280×800, Small tile 440×280, Marquee 1400×560, all 24-bit PNG or JPEG, no alpha.
  • Keep the voice charismatic and dash-free; scrub any real customer names from screenshots (use the demo thread).
