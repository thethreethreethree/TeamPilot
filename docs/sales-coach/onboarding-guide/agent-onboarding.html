Sales Coach · Setup Guide

Onboarding your sales agents

A step-by-step guide for a new client admin: how to bring your sales reps into the Sales Coach, give them access, and get them running the browser extension on their own conversations.

Who this is for: the person who set up your company's Sales Coach account (the admin). Time: about 5 minutes for you, plus a minute per agent for them to install the extension. The short version: create your account → add each agent by email → they accept the invite → you give them the Staff role → they install the extension and sign in.

Before you start: your admin account

Your company's Sales Coach account is created once, by whoever redeems your access key:

0

Redeem your access key

Go to elostate.com/redeem, enter the access key ELOSTATE gave you, and confirm it shows Sales Coach. Then enter your Company name, email, and a password (a name is optional). This creates your company and makes you its admin. You only do this once — your agents do not each need a key; you invite them (below).

Add your sales agents

1

Open the Team page

Sign in at elostate.com, then go to Sales Coach → Team (/dashboard/sales-coach/team). This page is admin-only — it's where you grant and manage who uses the Sales Coach.

2

Invite each agent by email

Click + Add member. In the Invite member dialog:

  • Enter the agent's work email.
  • Pick a Role. For a sales rep, choose Member. (This is their company-wide role. Only a company admin can invite someone as CEO/COO.)
  • Click Create invitation. This generates a unique, expiring invite link.
3

Send the invite link

The system does not auto-send email — you deliver the link so it comes from a real, trusted address. After the invite is created you get two options:

  • Send by email — opens your own mail app with the agent's address, subject, and link already filled in. Just hit send.
  • Copy link — copies the invite URL (elostate.com/invite/<code>) so you can paste it into Slack, a DM, or a text.

Click Invite another to add more agents, or Done when finished. Invite links expire in about two weeks; you can revoke or reissue one from the Team page.

4

Your agent activates their account

When the agent opens the invite link, they'll:

  • New to ELOSTATE: enter their email + a password (at least 6 characters), optionally their name, and click Create account & accept.
  • Already have an ELOSTATE login on that email: choose Sign in instead, then Sign in & accept.

On accept, they're attached to your company and land in the dashboard. (If their email provider requires confirmation, they confirm via the email link, then reopen the invite.)

5

Give each agent the Sales Coach role

Back on Sales Coach → Team, each agent now appears in the roster. On their row, tap the role button:

  • Staff — can use the Sales Coach (this is what a rep needs).
  • Admin — can also manage the team and roles.
  • — (dash) — removes Sales Coach access.

It saves immediately. This step is required: being a company member is not enough — an agent must be set to Staff (or Admin) here before the extension will work for them.

Two roles, kept separate (why there are two)

The Elostate role (Member / Lead / CEO / COO) is a company-wide access level set at invite time. The Sales Coach role (Staff / Admin) is set on the Team page and controls the coach specifically. They're independent on purpose — a company admin is not automatically a coaching user, and vice-versa. For a normal rep: invite as Member, then assign Staff.

Your agents: install the extension & sign in

Once an agent has an account with the Staff role, they install the browser extension. Send them to elostate.com/extension/download-sales — it has these five steps (about a minute):

#StepWhat they do
1Download & unzipClick Download the extension, then unzip it into a folder they'll keep (e.g. Documents/sales-coach-extension). Don't delete the folder — the browser loads from it.
2Open the extensions pageGo to chrome://extensions (Chrome/Brave) or edge://extensions (Edge).
3Turn on Developer modeFlip the Developer mode switch, top-right.
4Load unpackedClick Load unpacked and select the unzipped folder (the one with manifest.json). The Sales Coach icon appears in the toolbar.
5Pin it & sign inPin Sales Coach (puzzle-piece icon), open any conversation (Gmail, WhatsApp Web, LinkedIn, Slack…), click the Sales Coach icon, then Sign in — it connects to their workspace in one click.

A one-click Chrome Web Store install is coming; until then this download-and-load takes about a minute. Works in Chrome, Edge, and Brave.

If an agent gets stuck

  • "Your plan doesn't include it" on a tool → they haven't been given access. Assign them Staff on the Team page (Step 5).
  • Panel didn't appear? It doesn't open on its own — click the Sales Coach toolbar icon. It also can't open on browser system pages (chrome://), the Web Store, or PDFs — try a normal website.
  • Asked to sign in again / "session expired"? Click Sign in in the panel again and finish on the connect page — it hands the session back to the extension.
  • "Couldn't reach <host>"? Make sure they're on the official web address, not a preview link. If your team runs a custom address, they can clear it or point the extension at the right one.
