# Phone & video pitch rubric — DRAFT for approval (decision D6)

**Status: draft. Nothing is scored with this until you approve it.** Today every phone and video call is scored with
the door rubric (`attfiber-v1`, src/lib/coach/pitchScore/rubric.ts) and the score panel says so. You chose on
2026-11-22 to have a phone/video version drafted for your review.

**How it was drafted.** From the door rubric itself, element by element. The six sections and their weights are
unchanged (Introduction 12 · Discovery 16 · Consulting 14 · Close 15 · Transitions 8 · Delivery 35 = 100; bonuses capped
at +30; violations uncapped), so a remote score and a door score mean the same thing and the leaderboards stay
comparable. Only what cannot happen on a call is swapped (a truck in the street, getting inside the house, walking next
door); everything else is kept, with its "what counts" adjusted where a call changes how it lands. Every change is marked
and gives its reason, so you can accept, edit or reject each line.

Legend: **KEEP** unchanged · **ADAPT** same point, call wording · **SWAP** door-only item replaced.

## Base — 100

### Introduction — 12

| | Door element (pts) | Phone/video element | Pts | What counts | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SWAP | Trucks / neighborhood notice (3) | **Reason for the call** | 3 | A clear, specific reason in the first 20 seconds ("fiber just became available at your address") | No trucks on a call; the hook is why you're calling *them*, now |
| KEEP | Who and what (3) | Who and what | 3 | AT&T Fiber, connecting the neighbors | — |
| ADAPT | What we've done (3) | What's new at their address | 3 | Fiber is live / old lines replaced where they live | Same point, said about their address rather than the street |
| SWAP | Why we're here (3) | **Permission and agenda** | 3 | Asks for a few minutes and says what the call will cover ("two quick questions, then I'll show you…") | On a call the prospect can hang up at any second; earning the next minute is the job the door's "why we're here" did |

### Delivery — 35

| | Element | Pts | What counts (call wording) |
|---|---|---|---|
| KEEP | Objection handling | 8 | Acknowledge, isolate, answer, return to the close |
| KEEP | Talk / listen balance | 7 | The prospect gets real airtime |
| ADAPT | Tone and certainty | 7 | Confident and conversational — on a call the voice carries everything, so energy drops and reading from a script count against it |

## Violations (deducted after base + bonus)

| | Violation | Deduction | Trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| KEEP | Rude, dismissive or condescending | −10 (flags for manager review) | Any instance |
| KEEP | Talking too much | −5 | Rep talk share above ~75%, or a monologue over ~90 s with no prospect input |
| KEEP | Not asking enough questions | −5 | Fewer than 3 discovery questions before the checklist |
| KEEP | Talking over the prospect | −2 each (max −6) | Cuts in mid-sentence — more common on calls with delay, so it matters more here |
| KEEP | Ignoring a question | −2 each | The prospect asks and the rep moves on |

## Same rules as the door rubric

- A pitch counts toward the leaderboard once it reaches Discovery and scores at least 40 base points.
- Bills, speeds, promo prices and timelines are graded for whether the point was made, never for the numbers.
- D5 applies: under 100 rep words, a call isn't scored.

## What happens when you approve

1. It becomes `attfiber-remote-v1` — a new version, so door scores and any remote scores already given keep their own
   meaning (a score always states the rubric it was made under).
2. Phone and video calls are scored with it from then on; the "scored with the door rubric" note goes away.
3. Edits you make here become the rubric exactly — the point values are tested to add up to 100.
