EloState Sales CoachRep onboarding guide

Get set up in two parts

Create your EloState account on the web, then install the beta app on your iPhone or iPad. Follow the steps in order.

1Create your account Web
2Install the app iPhone / iPad
Part 1

Create your EloState account

On any browser. Takes about 10 minutes.

  1. 1

    Go to elostate.com

    Open elostate.com in your browser.

  2. 2

    Click Request access

  3. 3

    Enter your email and create a password

    Save these. This is your EloState login, and you'll use it to sign in to the app in Part 2.

  4. 4

    Complete the questionnaire with the Sales Trainer

    Answer the questions about your company and your personal goals. The coach uses these to tailor your sessions.

  5. ✓

    Ask your Manager or Admin to add you to their team

    Send them a message. Part 1 is done once they confirm you're on the team list.

Part 2

Install the EloState app

iPhone or iPad. The app is in beta, so it installs through Apple's TestFlight instead of the App Store.

Use the same Apple ID everywhere. The iCloud email you send your manager must be the Apple ID signed in on the phone you're installing on. A mismatch is the most common reason the install fails.
  1. 1

    Send your iCloud email to your manager Rep

  2. 2

    Manager forwards it to John Ramos Manager

    John grants access to the beta app. Reps don't need to do anything here but wait.

  3. 3

    Download TestFlight from the App Store

    It's a free Apple app. Install it now so it's ready for step 7.

  4. 4

    Accept the App Store Connect invite

    Look for an email from Apple Developer titled "You've been invited to App Store Connect." Tap Accept invitation.

    This invite expires after 3 days. If it expires, ask your manager to request a new one from John, then come back to this step.

  5. 5

    Sign in with your iCloud / Apple ID

  6. 6

    Confirm you land on the Apple Developer Account page

    This means the invite went through. Don't click "Enroll today". You don't need a paid developer account.

  7. 7

    Open the TestFlight email on your phone

    A second email arrives from Moses Maniquiz via TestFlight: "Moses Maniquiz has invited you to test Elostate Sales Coach." Tap View in TestFlight, or type the redeem code from the email into the TestFlight app.

  8. 8

    Tap Install in TestFlight

    The EloState app downloads to your phone or iPad.

  9. ✓

    Log in with your EloState account

    Use the email and password from Part 1, not your iCloud password.

You're set up

Once you're logged in, this is your home screen. Start a coaching session, log doors in Macro Mode, and track your progress.

If something goes wrong

No invite emailCheck spam and promotions. Then confirm with your manager that the right iCloud email went to John.
Invite expiredAsk your manager to request a new one from John, then restart Part 2 at step 4.
View in TestFlight does nothingMake sure TestFlight is installed and you're signed in with the same Apple ID on that device.
Can't log in to the appYou're probably using your iCloud password. Use your EloState password from Part 1.

Questions? Contact your Manager or Admin.
