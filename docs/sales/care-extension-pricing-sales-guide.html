Feature value & objection handling

What they're paying for — and how to defend the price.

The $24 buys six tools, on every channel:

SummarizeCatch up on a long thread in one glance — plus similar past resolutions worth reusing.
DissectDiagnose the real problem under the message (problem, evidence, root cause, outside view).
Ask CoachGrade a draft against the communication books; get a revision with the named principle and why.
AI Co-PilotDraft the reply AND name the move it made — so the agent learns, not just copies.
FormulateAgent types their intent in a line; it's shaped into a warm, on-brand reply. Agent stays in control.
Spawn taskTurn a conversation into a tracked C.A.R.E task without leaving the page.

Objection handling:

$24 a seat is a lot on top of our help desk.
Reframe to ROI: if it saves each agent 20 minutes a day, that's ~$300/mo of time — you're capturing under 10% of that. And it works across every channel your help desk doesn't cover. Start the free trial on your busiest agent and watch one week.
We already have AI in Zendesk / Intercom.
Those only work inside their own walls. Your agents also live in Gmail, Slack, and WhatsApp — this works everywhere, and it coaches the human instead of just deflecting tickets. It complements the suite; it doesn't compete with it.
Couldn't an agent just use ChatGPT?
ChatGPT doesn't know your product, invents details, and coaches nothing. C.A.R.E is grounded in the customer's own product context and the communication books, and it never fabricates — if it can't answer, it says so. That's the difference between a demo and a tool you trust in front of customers.
The close

Lead with the free trial, land on their existing inboxes, and expand to full C.A.R.E. The extension is the wedge — cheap to say yes to, impossible to give back once agents rely on it.
